Hello! I am a PhD student in Public Policy at the University of Chicago. My research focuses on the causal impact of institutional decisions on sexual and gender-based violence. I study how these institutions-- including criminal justice systems, media, and civil society-- shape violence and the social norms that sustain it. My work uncovers the structural factors that drive perpetration, providing evidence to inform the design of scalable policies for its reduction.

In addition to my own research, I have extensive work experience in academia as well as international organisations. I have contributed to various experimental and quasi-experimental studies on how cultural norms contribute to gender equity and violent crime. I have also worked on field experiments in women’s health, overseeing data collection and intervention implementation.

If you'd like to chat or collaborate, please feel free to reach out to me at sganapathi@uchicago.edu!
